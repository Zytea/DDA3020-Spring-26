Key corrections:

- Q1.4 only selects B. Option C says "KL divergence is positive and non-symmetric", which is strictly false. The correct PPT statement is that KL divergence is non-negative and generally non-symmetric.
- Q1.6 selects C. The PPT says kernel SVM is introduced for non-linearly separable data by replacing inner products with kernels; the option should not be rejected by adding an extra "all nonlinear data" condition.
- Q1.13 does not select A. The PPT statement is that fixing Sigma as the identity matrix gives a soft version of K-means, not the standard hard-assignment K-means algorithm.
- Q1.14 follows the PPT/exam simplified convention and selects C,D. Mathematically, the existence or number of exact solutions also depends on rank and consistency.
